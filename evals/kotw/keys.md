# Answer keys

Do not show this file to an agent whose analysis you are about to score.

Each key summarizes what the r/dominion Kingdom of the Week thread
concluded, weighted toward commenters who gave concrete lines or reported
playing the board. A thread is a handful of opinions, not ground truth. Where
commenters disagreed, both sides are listed.

Threads: `https://old.reddit.com/r/dominion/comments/<thread>/`, with the
thread id from `kingdoms.json`.

## k1: Butcher / City Quarter / Tunnel (pn49ed)

- Best-supported plan: a City Quarter engine. Open Butcher / Night Watchman,
  Butcher the Cursed Gold into a second Butcher, buy a City Quarter nearly
  every turn, and use Night Watchman to put a City Quarter on top of the deck
  each turn. One commenter reported 8 Provinces on turn 11 playing solo.
- Payload: one or two Tunnels and Embassies make Golds, and Butcher turns the
  Golds into Provinces.
- Faithful Hound adds Action density for City Quarter and is cheap to
  Butcher into. One or two is enough.
- Disputed: Merchant Guild as payload (one for, one firmly against), Fool's
  Gold (it stops City Quarter's draw).
- Also proposed, with less support: Night Watchman / Tunnel / Embassy money.
- Must find: Night Watchman setting up City Quarter. Tunnel Gold into Butcher.

## k2: Scrying Pool / Vineyard / Contraband (qab4vv)

- Open Hermit / Potion and build a Scrying Pool engine with Fortress and
  Hunting Grounds. Madman is worth taking early.
- Contraband is the only +Buy. Keep two payload targets (Province and
  Vineyard) so one named card doesn't shut you out, and expect the opponent
  to name Scrying Pool early.
- Golem is bad and Quarry is weak with so little +Buy. Big Money was called
  unplayable. Keep the Necropolis.
- Disputed: Monument (one commenter builds around several per turn, another
  calls it slow), and one Contraband versus two.
- Must find: Vineyard scaling with the same Actions Scrying Pool wants.
  Contraband needing two targets.

## k3: Collection / Experiment / Tiara (xc05mq)

- Collection + Experiment is the board: about 5 Collections and 5 Tiaras,
  and buy out the Experiment pile every turn for VP tokens. The Collection
  split matters as much as the Province split does elsewhere.
- Nothing costing more than $5 is worth gaining. Lackeys is the support
  draw, with Ports behind it.
- Proposed opening: Raze / Silver, then Silos (disputed: Tiara topdecking
  makes Silos matter only briefly).
- Disputed: an Artificer / Scholar build. Most replies thought it too slow.
- Must find: Collection scoring off Experiment's double gain. Tiara doubling
  Collection.

## k4: Worker's Village / Patrol / Black Cat (rf58en)

- A standard engine: Worker's Village, Patrol for draw, Grand Market for
  +Buy and money, Way of the Goat for thinning.
- Invest on Worker's Village (and on Grand Market) is strong and
  timing-sensitive.
- Black Cat matters most once greening starts. Use Patrol, Cellar or
  Cartographer to line Cats up for the opponent's Victory gains.
- Proposed openings: Silver / Black Cat, or Invest on Worker's Village plus
  Cellar. Few Silvers are wanted.
- Disputed: Animal Fair (one calls it bad because every terminal should be a
  Patrol, another buys it by trashing a spare Action), Cellar.
- Called weak: Trader, Cardinal, Cartographer, Gardens.
- Must find: Invest on the village pile. Black Cat as an endgame weapon.

## k5: Sea Witch / Wharf / Trail (witniz)

- Sea Witch early. Commenters split on whether the Curse race or the Trail
  split matters more, but both matter.
- Actions come from Trail (discarded to Warehouse, or gained by Weaver) and
  Crossroads, so the board is not "no village".
- Openings proposed: Salvager / Silver, Salvager / Warehouse, Weaver /
  Warehouse, and Sea Witch / Crossroads on 5/2.
- Berserker was called bad by three commenters: its attack triggers the
  opponent's Weaver and Trail.
- Trashing is slower than it looks. A three-pile ending on Curses, Trails
  and a third pile is likely, and one commenter argued for ending fast
  rather than building toward Provinces.
- Must find: Warehouse + Trail as Actions. Berserker helping the opponent.
  The three-pile risk.

## k6: Courtier / Mandarin / Village Green (ub77bx)

- The best-argued position: this is a Mandarin board. A Courtier + Mandarin
  deck keeps Village Green on top of the deck for Courtier to reveal, floods
  with Gold, and reuses the Golds within a turn. The commenter claimed $118
  and 8 Buys on turn 11 uncontested, and did not spell the line out.
- The same commenter called Displace too slow as a finisher (Displace +
  Gamble + an unplayed Gold costs more than buying the Province) and
  Cardinal useless.
- The other position: thin with Steward, use Gamble with Village Green and
  Tunnel, and Displace Golds into Provinces.
- Must find: Courtier revealing Village Green. Mandarin as a centerpiece
  that puts Village Green back on the deck each turn. How the Golds get
  replayed is our guess, not the thread's: gaining a Mandarin puts the
  Treasures in play back on the deck, and Gamble can play them again.
