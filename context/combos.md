# Known Combos

Two-card combinations strong enough to decide a game on their own, mostly
from Adam Horton's [Dominion Combos](https://adamhorton.com/flog/dominion-combos/)
and the community consensus behind it. When one is on the board, the
default plan is to build it or to stop the opponent from building it.
Check card text first: second editions removed some pieces.

## Game-deciding

- **Hermit + Market Square**: skip buying, so Hermit trashes itself for a
  Madman, and each Market Square in hand reacts to the trash by gaining a
  Gold. Needs about 5–6 Hermits, so the Hermit split decides mirrors.
- **Hunting Grounds + Lurker**: Lurker trashes Hunting Grounds from the
  Supply, and each trashed Hunting Grounds gains you a Duchy or 3 Estates.
  It reliably ends the game by about turn 11 with roughly 27–33 VP.
- **Donate + Market Square**: Donate trashes many cards at once, and each
  trash triggers every Market Square in hand. Stronger than Hermit + Market
  Square.

## Very strong

- **Bridge + Native Village**: store Bridges on the Native Village mat,
  then play them all in one turn. Each Bridge adds a Buy and cuts costs
  further, so one turn can take most of the Provinces.
- **Bridge + Royal Carriage or Flagship**: replay Bridge many times in one
  turn. Bridge with about 6 Royal Carriages empties the Provinces around
  turns 12–15.
- **Guildhall + Beggar or Delve**: cheap Treasure gains each give Coffers,
  which fund a huge turn.
- **Baths + Salt the Earth**: buying Salt the Earth scores VP without
  gaining a card, so Baths keeps paying out, and trashing Provinces from the
  Supply speeds up the ending.

## First edition only

These combos use cards removed in second editions, so they appear only on
older boards and in Dominion Online's first-edition sets: Capital +
Mandarin, Counting House + Night Watchman, Counting House + Travelling Fair,
Guildhall + Masterpiece.

## Playing against a combo

- Count how many copies of the key card the combo needs. Denying that
  number, by contesting the pile, is the main counter.
- If you can't deny it, race it: work out when it finishes and whether a
  faster plan or a three-pile ending gets there first.
